Table Landmarks

Each picture is one fixed camera framing, with players blurred out by a median over time. Click points along each edge where the blue or white playing surface meets the rail. Two well-spread points per edge are enough, and the edges can run off the picture. Left, right and far are as this camera sees the table. Then click the two posts of each goal you can see. Once enough edges are in, the table grid is drawn on top so you can check it sits on the table.
